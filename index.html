MAK SYNTH

A hands-on synthesizer kit for learning sound design. Built to teach the engineering behind electronic music.

Set up your synth

What you build

  1. The Keys

    Twelve keys that play a C major scale, from C up to the G above the next C. The engine moves them across four octaves, and you can hold all twelve at once.

  2. The Knobs

    Four that shape the volume of each note over time through attack, decay, sustain, and release. Two that shape a resonant low-pass filter through cutoff and resonance.

  3. The Circuit Board

    All the keys sit on a board wired to an Arduino. It reads every press and turn and sends it to your computer over USB.

  4. The Sound Engine

    On your computer, SuperCollider turns each of those messages into sound. Each key becomes a sound with its own oscillator, filter, and envelope.

  5. The Visualizers

    An oscilloscope, a spectrum analyzer, and an envelope graph react as you play, so you can see what each knob does to the sound.
